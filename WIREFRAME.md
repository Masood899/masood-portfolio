# MasoodKhan.me wireframe specification

## Desktop (≥ 1024px)

1. Sticky 78px navigation: MK. wordmark; Work, About, Experience, Contact; résumé action.
2. Hero in a two-column grid: role/location and headline/copy/actions on left; abstract systems illustration on right.
3. Professional summary strip with short editorial statement and location.
4. Selected Work: one large feature card for Zubayda By A3, then three cards for Unisys Research, MetaRecom and LULC.
5. Additional Builds: confirmed public GitHub repositories, kept visually secondary.
6. About: narrative on the right, education summary metrics on left.
7. Technical Expertise: four numbered rows for software, frontend, AI/ML, data/infrastructure.
8. Experience & Education: vertical timeline.
9. Recognition: two hackathon placements.
10. Contact band and dark footer.

## Mobile (≤ 760px)

1. Compact 68px navigation with MK. wordmark and menu button.
2. Hero becomes a single column: label, headline, copy, actions, location, then abstract visual.
3. Professional summary strip stacks below the hero.
4. Selected Work cards become full-width, one per row.
5. Additional Builds become a single-column list.
6. About, expertise, experience and recognition stack vertically.
7. Contact band and footer stack into a single column.

## Interaction / motion

- Small opacity + vertical reveal on section entry.
- Underline transition for navigation links.
- Subtle project-card lift and arrow rotation on hover.
- Mobile menu opens in document flow.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential movement.

## Visual rules

- Warm cream background, charcoal text, muted olive accent, stone dividers.
- Instrument Serif for editorial emphasis; Geist for interface/body; Geist Mono for micro-labels.
- No portrait, no stock photography, no fabricated project screenshots, no unsupported performance claims.
